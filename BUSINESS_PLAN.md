# FiveMinuteEdge — Business Plan
*Last updated: 2026*

---

## The Opportunity

Mid-level SaaS sales reps are the largest and most underserved segment in sales training. They've outgrown beginner content, can't justify $5,000 bootcamps, and are too busy for hour-long courses. They plateau at $600k–$1M quota attainment and stay there for years — not for lack of effort, but because nobody has built training that fits their life and addresses their specific failure mode.

That failure mode is value selling. The inability to quantify a prospect's problem, build a business case, and justify investment in the language of the economic buyer. This kills more deals than bad products, bad pricing, or bad competition combined.

FiveMinuteEdge is a daily microlearning app built specifically for this person. Five minutes a day. Field-tested curriculum from someone who's lived on both sides — top seller and sales leader. No theory. No fluff. Just the thing that changes how you show up in your next call.

---

## Product

**Format:** Daily lessons delivered as a web app, accessible on any device.

**Lesson structure (5 minutes):**
- Story — a real deal situation the learner recognizes
- Insight — the lesson extracted from that story
- Action — one specific thing to do that day
- Quiz — spaced repetition to lock in retention

**Curriculum:**
- Module 1: The Value Mindset (Weeks 1–2, 10 lessons) — *available at launch*
- Module 2: Uncovering Real Pain (Weeks 3–4)
- Module 3: Quantifying Value (Weeks 5–6)
- Module 4: Navigating the Buying Committee (Weeks 7–8)
- Module 5: Controlling the Process (Weeks 9–10)
- Module 6: Closing Like a Top Performer (Weeks 11–12)

**Engagement mechanics:**
- Daily lesson unlock (creates scarcity and habit)
- Streak tracking (creates identity and consistency)
- Progress dashboard (creates completion motivation)
- Morning email reminder (creates the habit trigger)

## Market

**Target user:** Mid-level SaaS AEs, 3–8 years experience, $800k–$2M quota, stuck below 85% attainment.

**Market size:**
- ~2.8M SaaS sales reps in the US
- ~40% are mid-level AEs by experience band = ~1.1M addressable users
- Conversion target for Year 1: 500 paying users
- Long-term target: 25,000 paying users

**Market tailwind:** Sales training software is growing at 15%+ CAGR. The microlearning segment is growing fastest. No incumbent is purpose-built for complex B2B sales at this experience level and price point.

## Business Model

**Pricing:** $19/month (individual self-serve)
- 7-day free trial, no credit card required
- Monthly recurring, cancel anytime
- Annual plan at $159/year (~30% discount) introduced at 500 users

**Unit economics:**
- Monthly revenue per user: $19
- Stripe fee: ~$0.85 (2.9% + $0.30)
- Net revenue per user: ~$18.15
- Target churn: <5% monthly (industry benchmark for sticky daily-habit apps)

## Go-to-Market

**Phase 1 — Seed (Months 1–3): Find 50 paying users**

The goal is not growth. The goal is proof. Fifty paying users who complete lessons, maintain streaks, and can tell you in their own words what changed in how they sell.

Channels:
- LinkedIn content — post one insight per day from the curriculum. The target audience is on LinkedIn daily. Build in public.
- Direct outreach — identify 200 mid-level AEs on LinkedIn, send personalized cold messages offering free trial access in exchange for feedback.
- Sales communities — post genuinely helpful content in Pavilion, RevGenius, and r/sales. No pitching — just value.

**Phase 2 — Growth (Months 4–12): Scale to 500 users**

Channels:
- Referral program — existing users get one free month for every paying referral
- LinkedIn ads — target SaaS AEs by title, company size, and seniority. $500/month budget, optimizing for trial signups
- Podcast appearances — pitch sales-focused podcasts (30 Minutes to President's Club, The Sales Evangelist, Make It Happen Mondays)
- Newsletter sponsorships — sponsor sales-focused newsletters with 10k+ subscribers

**Phase 3 — Expansion (Year 2): Team licenses**

At 500+ users with proven retention, introduce team pricing:
- Team plan: $15/user/month (minimum 5 seats) — sold to Sales Enablement leaders
- This unlocks a B2B motion alongside the self-serve individual motion
- Manager dashboard showing team progress and completion rates

## Competitive Advantage

**Why this wins:**

1. **Specificity** — built for one person (mid-level SaaS AE) with one problem (value selling). Generalist platforms can't compete on specificity.

2. **Authorship credibility** — content built by someone who was a top seller AND a sales leader. Not an academic. Not a consultant. Someone who's closed the deals and watched others fail at them.

3. **Format** — the daily habit format creates retention that course platforms can't match. A rep who completes Day 1 is likely to come back for Day 2. Completion rates on traditional courses are 3–5%. Daily habit apps retain at 30–40%.

4. **Price** — at $19/month, this is a self-serve decision for any employed sales rep. No manager approval, no budget cycle, no procurement. The barrier to trial is zero.
